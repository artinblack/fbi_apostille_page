FAQ

Common questions.

If your destination country is a Hague Convention member (120+ countries — South Korea, Italy, Germany, France, Spain etc.) you need an apostille. For non-Hague countries like Saudi Arabia and China, you need full embassy legalization instead. Not sure? Send us a photo and we'll confirm for free.
The FBI is a federal agency, so its documents must be apostilled by the US Department of State — not a state Secretary of State office. Many services get this wrong. Sending your FBI background check to the wrong authority will result in rejection. We process this correctly, every day.
No travel and no mailing required for most clients. The majority of our customers submit their FBI background check electronically — simply email, WhatsApp, or upload your PDF through our order form and we handle the rest. If your specific situation requires an original hard-copy document, we'll let you know during your free review and provide full instructions. We hand-deliver to the DOS on your behalf and deliver your certified apostille digitally or via FedEx/DHL worldwide.
Expedited processing is 8–9 business days from when we receive your document. Standard processing takes 3–5 weeks. For truly urgent needs, we offer same-day certified digital scan delivery once the apostille is complete.
