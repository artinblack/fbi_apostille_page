About This Service

Apostille on FBI Criminal Background Check

If you need to submit your FBI background check in South Korea, Italy, Spain, or any other Apostille-verified country, we handle the entire apostille process on your behalf. As a licensed third-party agency based in Washington DC, we submit your documents in person to the US Department of State, the only federal agency authorized to apostille FBI background checks.

Most clients complete the process entirely online by submitting a PDF. No travel to DC, no mailing required in most cases. We review your document for free, confirm what's needed, and give you an exact quote before you commit.
